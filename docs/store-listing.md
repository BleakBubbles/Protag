## Short description (80 max)

Store and play your songs in a more organized manner using tags.

## Full description (4,000 max)

Protag is a music player for those who love organizing. Some of its features include:

• Tags instead of playlists
• Tags inside tags
• Tagging many songs at once
• Shuffling any mix of tags, artists and albums
• Saved mixes that stay up to date
• Sleep timer
• Light and dark themes
• Backups of your tags

Protag plays the audio files already on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no tracking, and nothing leaves your phone.
