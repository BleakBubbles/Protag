# Protag privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Protag is a music player for the music files already on your phone. Everything it does happens on your phone. It has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't send anything about you or your music anywhere.

## What Protag reads

- **Your audio files.** With your permission (Android's "Music and audio" access), Protag reads the audio files on your phone and their details, such as title, artist, album, genre and album art, so it can list and play them. You can choose which folders it reads.
- **Files you choose.** If you import playlists or restore a backup, Protag reads only the files or folder you pick.

## What Protag keeps

Protag keeps the following on your phone, in its own storage:

- your tags, mixes and their settings;
- how many times songs have been played and when, for orders like "Most played";
- your preferences, such as theme and sort orders.

None of this leaves your phone unless you export it yourself. Uninstalling Protag deletes it. Clearing the app's data in Android settings does the same.

## Backups you make

When you export a tag backup, Protag writes a file to the folder you pick, or hands it to the app you choose from Android's share sheet. From there it's handled by whatever you saved or sent it to. Protag doesn't upload backups anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Music and audio:** to find and play your music.
- **Notifications:** to show playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen.
- **Foreground service (media playback):** to keep playing while the screen is off or you're in another app.
- **Vibration:** for small haptic feedback when you tap and drag.

## What Protag doesn't do

- It doesn't collect, share or sell personal data.
- It doesn't use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
- It doesn't connect to the internet to send or receive your data.
- It doesn't use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

## Children

Protag isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, since it collects no information at all.
